Module simplify 

Source
Expand description

Peephole simplification of hole-free expressions, run over the final program (domain axioms, predicates, functions and methods).

Composing independently encoded snapshot operations produces wrap/unwrap round trips: reference snapshots built and immediately dereferenced, generic casts cancelled out, snapshot constructors compared field by field. The pass undoes them with equations, listed by the kind of node they rewrite, each of which can be checked on its own. The traversal applies them bottom-up and takes care of everything that depends on the context of a node:

  • let-bound locals are replaced by their values where that means the same: inside an old(..) or unfolding only locals and constants (other values may depend on the heap or the permissions held), and not values whose locals a binder (a quantifier or let) rebinds,
  • the span of the root of an expression tree is kept, since Prusti backtranslates Viper errors through it,
  • quantifier triggers and magic wands are left as encoded.

Equations may drop subexpressions together with their well-definedness checks: Prusti checks all side conditions explicitly in impure code.
