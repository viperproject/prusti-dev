JoinOwnedReadAndWriteCapabilities

When joining an owned place where one place has capability Read and the other has capability Write, all borrowed expansions of the place with Read capability should be labelled.

In particular, the Read capability place could be a shared reference that is dereferenced. The place will not have any capability after the join, but reborrows derived from the dereference may still have capability.
